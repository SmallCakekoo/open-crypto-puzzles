## Why this matters

- **"Question: Final version of the second chapter of Wattpad story"** is the
  first direct, unambiguous confirmation that the source is exactly that
  chapter — previously only inferred from the Reddit discussion thread.
- **"I wanted to remove one of the twists I had. The block is now slightly
  easier... hashed with two line breaks between paragraphs now"** confirms two
  things at once: (a) the separator changed from the original to a doubled
  form between the two hash attempts, and (b) there was a separate "twist"
  removed, whose content is not stated here. See `analysis/leads.md` for the
  investigation into what "twist" could mean, calibrated against the author's
  other uses of the word elsewhere in the series.
- **Both expiry conditions have passed** (Tanabata 2022; the 200-day Mayer
  Multiple has likely crossed $100/mbtc at some point since) with no
  disclosure ever posted — checked against her complete Reddit post history
  (100 submissions, confirmed complete via pagination), consistent with the
  escrow still being funded today.
