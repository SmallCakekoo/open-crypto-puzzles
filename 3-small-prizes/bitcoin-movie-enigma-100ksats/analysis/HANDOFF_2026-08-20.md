title "somehow" into an English BIP39 word -> 34 words. Drop exactly 10

`bc1q94ecsn0qk8lap2gefrycnms3ruepy889z969a6` (100,000 sats, funded 2022, still
unspent). Full rules and sources: `README.md`, `clues/author-posts.md`.

## What's confirmed vs. still open

**Confirmed / high confidence, don't re-litigate:**
- All 34 panel identities as listed above (user's own research; treat as ground
  truth unless new contrary evidence turns up).
- The oracle (`tools/oracle.py`) is correct and certified (`--selftest` passes
  against public BIP39/BIP84 vectors). It checks BIP84, BIP49, BIP44 (2 accounts x
  3 indices, receive/external chain only) plus 3 raw P2WPKH paths.
- **Verified fact about the oracle's own scope**: BIP49 addresses always start
  `3...` and BIP44 always `1...` -- neither can *ever* equal the bc1q target.
  Only BIP84 and the 3 raw paths can structurally match. The oracle also never
  tests the internal/change chain, only external/receive, and only accounts 0-1,
  indices 0-2. A companion script, `analysis/bruteforce_recheck_paths.py`, widens
  this (more accounts/indices, both chains) for any mnemonic already known to be
  BIP39-checksum-valid, without regenerating from scratch.
- Title-to-word rule that explains the clean majority of panels: **whole word >
  singular/plural > substring-within-a-single-word (no crossing spaces)**,
  validated mechanically against ~15+ known cases with zero counterexamples. Two
  corrections found late and already applied in `data/films.csv`: panel 25 "bar"
  IS a real substring of "Barry" (was wrongly "none"); panel 33 "shine" is NOT a
  literal substring of "shining" (needs silent-e restoration, an unvalidated
  transformation) -- corrected to "none".

**Open / actively being worked on:**
- **The intruder criterion.** H1 ("shares a director or lead actor with another
  panel here") was the leading hypothesis for the OLD dataset (gave exactly 10).
  Recomputed against the NEW corrected dataset
  (`analysis/intruder_repeat_check.py`, already updated): **gives 14, not 10**.
  Neither "director-only" (9) nor "actor-only" (8) sub-filter recovers 10 either.
  **H1 is falsified against the current dataset. This is the main open problem.**
- To find a replacement criterion, we started a systematic single-field IMDb
  audit (genre, country, runtime, writer, budget, etc.) -- see next section, this
  is IN PROGRESS and blocked on data collection.
- Word-level ambiguities not yet resolved even for keeper panels: several panels
  have 2-3 tied literal candidates with no rule to pick one (e.g. The Man in the
  Iron Mask: man/iron/mask). A few panels have zero literal candidate at all
  (Spartacus, Leon: The Professional, The Goonies, Sharknado, Raiders of the Lost
  Ark) -- but note several of these are *also* flagged by H1, so if H1 (or its
  eventual replacement) is right, they may never need a word at all.

## House rules this whole investigation has followed (keep following them)

- Never invent a word or accept a criterion because it "would be convenient" --
  every accepted candidate/criterion in this repo traces to either a literal
  substring check against the real 2048-word BIP39 list, or a sourced,
  independent piece of evidence (not asserted from memory).
- A checksum-valid BIP39 mnemonic is not evidence of anything by itself (~1/256 of
  random word combinations pass by chance). Only a derived-address match against
  the target counts as a solution.
- When the user provides a new identification or correction, verify what you can
  (reverse image search on the actual still, cross-check plot details against
  independent sources) before accepting it, and say plainly when something is
  "user's own claim, not independently verified by me" vs. "independently
  sourced."
- Document everything in `analysis/tested.md` (full negatives ledger) and
  `analysis/leads.md` (ranked open leads) as you go, including explicitly what a
  new finding invalidates and what it leaves standing.
